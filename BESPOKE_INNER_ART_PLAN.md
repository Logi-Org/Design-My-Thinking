# Bespoke inner-page art plan

This branch replaces the earlier reuse of homepage artwork with page-specific illustrations. Homepage assets remain on the homepage. Inner-page illustrations are purpose-built around the actual page content.

## Approach
Six bespoke scenes: interrogating the brief; designing the intervention; facilitating the live room; challenging the assumption that a workshop is always the answer; shifting participation modes; keeping momentum after the room empties.

## Musings
Four bespoke scenes tied directly to the four field notes: branching scenarios; a question nurturing new thinking; a canvas shaping attention; physical Post-its turning into an interactive browser tool. Expanded field notes reuse only their own matching illustration.

## About
Eight small belief-specific doodles plus two larger illustrations for “What I bring” and “How I show up”. The portrait remains the primary human image.

## Contact / Thank You
A messy problem being put on the table; looping conversation; competing perspectives converging; a real fork-in-the-road decision; and a bespoke message-received envelope illustration.

## Principles Lab
One bespoke trade-off / balance illustration in the shell. Interaction and data behaviour remain unchanged.

Visual rule: black hand-drawn ink dominates. Colour is sparse and meaningful: Post-its, one highlighted decision, or one focal lightbulb. Art has transparent backgrounds so it sits directly on the site paper.
