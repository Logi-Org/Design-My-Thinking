Prototype 01 · LinkedIn Conversation Engine

Find the conversations worth entering.

A small experiment in making LinkedIn more deliberate. Rather than starting with “what should I post?”, the engine starts with your themes, finds conversations where you may genuinely have something to add, helps you form a point of view, then drafts in a voice you can edit.

The idea in four moves.

1Sense: scan conversations against themes that matter to you.
2Decide: rank where there is a genuine contribution to make.
3Think: agree + extend, challenge, or reframe the conversation.
4Draft: turn the point of view into editable language. You still decide what gets posted.

What I was testing

Most AI writing tools make it easier to produce more content. I was more interested in whether a tool could help someone contribute less often, but with more relevance and a clearer point of view.

Signal

What deserves attention?

Use themes, freshness, conversation quality and contribution potential to filter down to a small number of worthwhile opportunities.

Thinking

What do I actually think?

Force a choice between extending, challenging or reframing before asking the model to write. Drafting comes after perspective.

Voice

Does it sound like a person?

Use source writing and editing history to learn patterns, while keeping every output editable and every LinkedIn action human-approved.
