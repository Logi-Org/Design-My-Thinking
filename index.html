How I work

Understand → Design → Do

Simple on purpose. The craft sits in how each stage responds to the people, problem and context.

01

Understand

Challenge the brief, hear different perspectives, expose assumptions and get specific about the outcome.

02

Design

Build the sequence, questions, artefacts and environment that help the group do the thinking themselves.

03

Do

Facilitate the room, adapt when reality disagrees with the plan, capture the thinking and land clear next steps.

↖ No, the first step isn't “write an agenda”.
