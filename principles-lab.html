Leadership Principles Tension Lab

You have $600. Every principle costs $100. Choose exactly six: two as the priorities you want the room to protect most fiercely, and four as supporting priorities. Then check the balance and discuss what your choices might amplify — or accidentally neglect.

This is a conversation device, not a scoring model.

The pairings and watch-outs below are deliberately provocative prompts. Their job is to create useful disagreement, not tell a leadership team what the “correct” principles are.
