About

Hello. I'm Daniel.

I spend an unreasonable amount of time thinking about how groups of people think — especially when the problem is complicated, the perspectives are different and a decision actually matters.

Facilitator. Designer. Professional question-asker.

A little context

I design the conditions for people to think better together.

I'm an enterprise leader with 15+ years' experience across transformation, customer experience and human-centred design. My work has included designing and delivering interventions for senior leaders confronting complex, far-reaching problems.

The common thread is not a particular workshop method. It is helping people create transparency, reduce complexity, build shared understanding and leave with greater clarity about what happens next.

Things I believe

A few working principles.

  • Outputs matter. Outcomes matter more.
  • A workshop is not an agenda.
  • Alignment does not require everyone to agree on everything.
  • People support what they have genuinely helped create.
  • Difficult conversations should not automatically be designed out of the room.
  • Methods are useful. Knowing why you're using them is more useful.
  • If reality disagrees with the agenda, change the agenda.
  • The most important workshop measure is what happens afterwards.
